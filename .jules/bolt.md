## 2024-05-24 - np.ndarray buffer offset alignment issues
**Learning:** While `np.ndarray(buffer=...)` avoids empty array creation overhead and view slicing, it throws a ValueError when operating on arrays with unaligned offsets in older numpy versions, or silently generates unaligned arrays that crash when used depending on the architecture/version. GGUF packs KV pairs back-to-back without padding, making unaligned offsets common. The PR `#37` approach (caching `np.dtype(dtype).itemsize` but keeping the `.view()` slicing) is safer and avoids the `ValueError` on unaligned offsets.
**Action:** The micro-optimization `np.ndarray(buffer=...)` should not be used in memory-mapped data parsing where offsets are unaligned. Stick to `np.dtype(dtype).itemsize` caching and `.view()` for slicing to ensure safety across all platforms and GGUF files.
